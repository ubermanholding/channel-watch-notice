CHANNEL WATCH · DATA-USE NOTICE · Updated October 26, 2026

How Channel Watch uses eBay data

Channel Watch is a private operational dashboard for its owner’s Shopify, eBay and AutoDS workflow. This notification service supports the owner’s eBay app; it does not provide a public view of the dashboard.

Data and purpose

With the seller’s authorization, Channel Watch reads active listing identifiers, titles, SKUs, prices and quantities, and recent order identifiers, order-line details, fulfillment status, deadlines and tracking. It uses those records to show inventory, order exceptions and estimated margins alongside separately supplied costs and fees.

Buyer names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, payment details, order notes and complete eBay responses are not saved in the dashboard. Data returned by the eBay APIs may be processed transiently to select the fields needed by the dashboard.

Access and storage

Dashboard records are stored privately for the signed-in owner in the application’s hosted database. App credentials and renewable seller tokens are encrypted on the server. The public receiver verifies eBay signatures using eBay’s official public keys. Its app credentials are encrypted separately. It retains a hashed account-deletion signal and delivery identifier until the private dashboard acknowledges processing; it does not store raw notice payloads or seller names. The dashboard checks its own deletion signal through a restricted authenticated request. The receiver has no dashboard access token and cannot read orders or inventory.

Data is used for this dashboard and its required service providers, including OpenAI Sites hosting and Cloudflare runtime/storage. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Retention and control

Successful complete syncs replace the integration’s previous snapshot. Failed updates retain the previous snapshot. Disconnecting removes the stored seller authorization while retaining a clearly disconnected snapshot. The seller can revoke access in eBay’s third-party permissions settings.

When eBay sends a valid signed account-deletion notice matching the connected or retained seller identity, Channel Watch removes that seller’s linked eBay snapshot and seller tokens during its next automatic check (normally hourly) or sync. Other channel data and alert rules remain. Unrelated or unverified notices cannot delete the owner’s data.
